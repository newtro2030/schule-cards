# Unit 2 – Stars: Thema und Schreiben

Zur Klassenarbeit am 23.11.:
- What makes someone a star?
- Examples of stars
- Advantages and disadvantages of being a star

## Lernkarten #Englisch/Unit-2-Stars

Q: What makes someone a star? Nenne vier Dinge auf Englisch.
A: z. B.
- **talent** – they can sing, act or play very well
- **hard work** – they practise a lot
- **lots of fans** – many people like them
- **success** – they win awards, sell many songs or tickets
- they are **on TV / in films / on social media**

Q: Welche Sätze beschreiben, was jemanden zu einem Star macht?
- [x] She has a lot of talent.
- [x] He practises every day.
- [x] Millions of fans follow her on social media.
- [ ] He never works hard.
- [ ] Nobody knows her name.

Q: Welche Arten von Stars gibt es? Nenne vier auf Englisch.
A: singers, actors / actresses, athletes (z. B. football players), influencers / YouTubers, musicians, bands

Q: Ordne zu: Wer ist was?
- [x] Taylor Swift is a singer.
- [x] Lionel Messi is a football player.
- [ ] Taylor Swift is a football player.
- [x] Emma Watson is an actress.
A: Taylor Swift ist Sängerin (USA), Lionel Messi Fußballspieler (Argentinien), Emma Watson Schauspielerin (Hermine in *Harry Potter*).

Q: Nenne drei Vorteile, ein Star zu sein (auf Englisch).
A: z. B.
- Stars are **rich** – they **earn a lot of money**.
- They **are famous** and have **lots of fans**.
- They can **travel** a lot / **go on tour**.
- They can do what they **love**.
- They **win awards**.

Q: Nenne drei Nachteile, ein Star zu sein (auf Englisch).
A: z. B.
- Stars have **no privacy** – photographers follow them.
- People **recognise** them **in public**.
- They are **under pressure** and get **criticism**.
- They have **little free time** – the work is **tiring**.
- They can feel **lonely**.

Q: Welche Sätze sind **Vorteile** (advantages) eines Stars?
- [x] You earn a lot of money.
- [ ] Photographers follow you everywhere.
- [x] You meet lots of interesting people.
- [ ] You have no private life.
- [x] Fans admire you.

Q: Welche Sätze sind **Nachteile** (disadvantages) eines Stars?
- [x] You can't go shopping without fans around you.
- [x] There is a lot of pressure.
- [ ] You can travel all over the world.
- [x] People write bad things about you on the internet.
- [ ] You win awards.

Q: Wie sagst du: „Meiner Meinung nach …“ / „Ich denke, …“
A: **In my opinion**, … / **I think** …

Q: Wie sagst du: „Einerseits … andererseits …“
A: **On the one hand**, … **On the other hand**, …

Q: Wie sagst du: „Ein Vorteil ist, dass …“ / „Ein Nachteil ist, dass …“
A: **One advantage is that** … / **One disadvantage is that** …

Q: Wie sagst du: „Ich möchte (nicht) berühmt sein, weil …“
A: I **would like** to be famous because … / I **wouldn't like** to be famous because …

Q: Wie sagst du: „Mein Lieblingsstar ist …“
A: **My favourite star is** …

Q: Wie sagst du: „Zum Schluss …“ / „Alles in allem …“
A: **In the end**, … / **All in all**, …

Q: Mit welchen Ausdrücken leitest du deine Meinung ein?
- [x] In my opinion, …
- [x] I think …
- [ ] Yesterday …
- [x] I believe …
- [ ] Last week …

Q: Beispieltext: Beschreibe einen Star in 5 Sätzen (Vorlage zum Nachmachen).
A: *Taylor Swift is a famous singer from the USA. She started singing when she was a child. She writes her own songs. She has won a lot of awards and she has given concerts all over the world. In my opinion, she is a good role model because she works very hard.*
- Simple Present: *is, writes*
- Simple Past: *started, was*
- Present Perfect: *has won, has given*

Q: Beispieltext: Wärst du gern ein Star? (Vorlage für die Meinung)
A: *On the one hand, being a star is great. You are rich and famous and you have lots of fans. On the other hand, you have no privacy and photographers follow you everywhere. In my opinion, I wouldn't like to be a star because I like my quiet life.*
